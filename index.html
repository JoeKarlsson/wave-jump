Wave Jump

A race for two players on one keyboard. Click the game, then press the spacebar to start. Player 1 jumps with W, steers with A and D, and dives with S. Player 2 uses the arrow keys. First one through the checkered gate scores.

Wave Jump

This game needs two players and a keyboard, so it won't work on a phone or tablet.

Open it on a computer: wavejump.joekarlsson.com
